## Context

What forced the decision. The constraints that were actually binding, not a general description of
the area. If a fact discovered during research is what made this necessary, state the fact.

## Decision

What was decided, in the imperative. One paragraph if possible.

## Consequences

What becomes true, including the bad parts. An ADR that lists only benefits is not being honest and
will be re-litigated by whoever hits the cost.

## Alternatives considered

Each with why it lost. "Not considered" is a valid entry and is more useful than a fabricated
comparison.

## Reversal cost

How expensive it is to change course later, and what would trigger reconsidering. This is the field
that decides whether an ADR was needed at all — cheap-to-reverse decisions should not have one.
